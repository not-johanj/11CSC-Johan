Climate Change

Climate change is the long-term warming of Earth's climate caused mainly by greenhouse gases released through human activities.

As temperatures rise, glaciers and ice sheets melt, adding more water to the oceans. Warmer oceans also expand, causing sea levels to rise around the world.

Rising sea levels increase flooding, coastal erosion, and the loss of habitats for both people and wildlife.
